# Judah's people shown as Black Israelites

- People with no portrait of their own were sometimes given a European-looking stand-in figure
  (Shobab, a son of David, among them). About 1,450 people drew one of those figures, 130 of them
  of the tribe of Judah. The four European-looking figures (two men, two women) and the two group
  pictures with European-looking men are redrawn as Black Israelites in the same clay style.
- The tribe of Judah's 316 people without a portrait now only ever get a Black figure, never the
  Native American one (`judahPeople.ts`, from the published People data).
- The 261 named portraits were checked and are unchanged: every Israelite among them is Black.
