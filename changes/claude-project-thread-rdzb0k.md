- Scripture Clips (preview): a new CyberJudah screen, not one of Bible Strong's, opened from More under 4 Chapters a Day. It plays a chapter as a Shorts style feed of the classes the Bible reader shows under its verses ("Taught from" and "Read in class"), in the reader's order: for each verse, the class the reader lists first, with verses the class takes up together sharing one clip. The class video plays in place and moves on when the class reaches its next moment, into the next chapter at the end. The title opens the reader's book and chapter picker. "More" opens a sheet like the verse panel, with the verses, the class, and links to the chapter, its study note, the class, and Share.
