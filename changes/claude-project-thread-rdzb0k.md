- Scripture Clips (preview): a new CyberJudah screen, not one of Bible Strong's, opened from More under 4 Chapters a Day. It plays a chapter as a Shorts style feed of the classes the Bible reader shows under its verses ("Taught from" and "Read in class"), one verse per clip. Each clip starts at the second the class reads the verse (from the captions, the chapter data's `vt`) and runs until the class reads its next verse; the class reading on keeps going, otherwise the class the reader lists first. Verses whose second the captions don't show fall back to where a class takes up the passage. Clips play by themselves as they come on screen, muted until a tap (as Shorts and Reels do); a tap on the video turns the sound on, then pauses and plays. The title opens the reader's book and chapter picker, and the next chapter follows the last clip. "More" opens a sheet like the verse panel.
