# New app icon: the owner's cyber lion

- The app icon is now the owner's own half-machine, half-gold lion from the cover of
  *Case Studies of the Bible*, on the book's look: near-black background, faint cyan grid,
  and the pink and cyan corner brackets. The owner picked this version ("L3") on 2026-10-31.
- `icon-2.png` (iOS icon, 1024, no transparency) and `icon.png` (the in-app copy shown on Ask,
  the sidebar and audio artwork) carry the new icon.
- Android adaptive icon: `foreground-image.png` is the lion inside the safe zone,
  `background-image.png` is the dark background with the grid (the brackets are left out because
  launchers crop the corners).
- The lion was sharpened from the book's 512-pixel copy to 2048 pixels before it was placed.
- A new app icon needs a native build; it ships with the next build, not over the air.
- Not changed: splash screens and the notification icon.
