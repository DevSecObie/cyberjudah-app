# Timeline cards no longer sit on top of each other

- The five American captivity periods (Into the Ships, The House of Bondage, Reconstruction and
  Jim Crow, Civil Rights and the Awakening, Redemption) placed many cards on the same row at the
  same time, so cards were drawn over one another (365 overlapping pairs; 186 in Reconstruction and
  Jim Crow alone). Each card keeps its published row when that row is free; only a card that would
  overlap moves to the nearest free row, and the period grows taller (scroll down) when it needs
  more rows. No event, date or text changes.
- Bible Strong's own periods keep their hand-placed rows exactly as before.
- A card with no picture yet no longer shows an empty grey slot at its end: its title and dates
  use the whole card. Cards with a picture are unchanged.
