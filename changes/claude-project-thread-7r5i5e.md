- The Bible reader shows the classes' mini videos, as the Telegram app does: after the last verse a class taught, a small deck of its recordings, and at the end of the chapter a deck of every class that taught it ("Taught from Genesis 49") or read it aloud ("Read in class"). They come from CyberJudah's published chapter data (data.cyberjudah.io), so new classes appear without an app update. A card plays the class from the moment it read the verse, and shows the teacher and date. The setting that hides Bible Strong's videos hides them too.
- The player page (embed/youtube.html) takes a start time.
