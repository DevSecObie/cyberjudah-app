## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Romans 6 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1, 6-8, 12-18, 20-23, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Romans 6:1](/bible/romans/6#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/romans/6#v1)</sup> What shall we say then? Shall we continue in sin, that grace may abound?

- “What shall we say then? Shall we continue in sin, that grace may abound?” That is the scary mindset of convincing yourself that grace covers everything, and it is a terrible mindset to have.
- It is the mindset of Christianity: I found grace, grace covers me, I can do what I want when I want; all I have to do is believe in Jesus, I don't have to do anything he says, just believe he is my Lord and saviour. Christianity is evil.
- When temptation hits you alone online late at night and nobody from the congregation would know, integrity is to cut it off immediately and fight the temptation, not to entertain it privately, watch a little and justify it later, or tell yourself grace covers everything. The real battlefield is private: many fall spiritually in secret long before they fall publicly. The representative shows up at the Sabbath, saluting hard and bowing all the way down, but the real you shows up at home. The brother who comes in righteous and goes home and verbally, financially or physically abuses his wife, and the sister who bows to the floor here and goes home and cusses her husband out, keeps a filthy house, doesn't cook, lack integrity and have terrible, poor character.

**[Romans 6:6](/bible/romans/6#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/romans/6#v6)</sup> Knowing this, that our old man is crucified with him, that the body of sin might be destroyed, that henceforth we should not serve sin.

- “Knowing this, that our old man is crucified with him, that the body of sin might be destroyed, that henceforth we should not serve sin.” The old man must die: the weed head, the crack head, the coke head, the adulterer. That old man has to go. You have to be reformed.

**[Romans 6:7](/bible/romans/6#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/romans/6#v7)</sup> For he that is dead is freed from sin.

- He that is dead is free from sin: he that destroyed the old man is free from sin. You cannot carry the old man and the new man; one of them has to die.

**[Romans 6:12](/bible/romans/6#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/romans/6#v12)</sup> Let not sin therefore reign in your mortal body, that ye should obey it in the lusts thereof.

- “Let not sin therefore reign in your mortal body.” Why? Because you got a war and don’t even realize it.
- If you struggling: here she go, ‘I’m just going to visit my ex cuz he just need a little something’, he’s going through a hard time, I got to talk to him. Brothers in New York and Atlanta and other schools, secretly in some of these ‘dumb chicken head sisters’. The ‘jump off queen’ wearing fringes don’t say nothing, he don’t say nothing, until he starts looking at another sister. Now she want to come all holy: he’s been by my house having sex with me and he won’t marry me. How long is this going on, sister? Three years. And now you are holy and righteous. ‘If you don’t get your scabby behind out of here, you and him.’
- If eternal life is through Jesus Christ, then do this: “Let not sin therefore reign in your mortal body, that ye should obey it in the lusts thereof.”
- “Let not sin therefore reign in your mortal body, that ye should obey it in the lusts thereof.” If your expectation is to be called a son of God in the kingdom, then don't let sin reign or rule in this mortal body. We have to have the word of God engrafted in our souls, so that sin does not reign in us and does not rule us.

**[Romans 6:13](/bible/romans/6#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/romans/6#v13)</sup> Neither yield ye your members as instruments of unrighteousness unto sin: but yield yourselves unto God, as those that are alive from the dead, and your members as instruments of righteousness unto God.

- “Neither yield ye your members as instruments of unrighteousness unto sin”: that’s what you doing. “But yield yourselves unto God”: we all got to come to that point and yield ourselves unto God, “as those that are alive from the dead.”
- “And your members as instruments of righteousness unto God”: our members, our arms, our mind, our thoughts, should be members of righteousness unto God.
- “Neither yield ye your members as instruments of unrighteousness unto sin: but yield yourselves unto God”. Do not yield yourself unto sin; yield your members “as instruments of righteousness unto God.” Apply righteousness in your life.
- “Neither yield ye your members as instruments of unrighteousness unto sin: but yield yourselves unto God, as those that are alive from the dead, and your members as instruments of righteousness unto God.” Don't yield your members to sin, because you are expecting the kingdom. That is what we are all doing this for.

**[Romans 6:14](/bible/romans/6#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/romans/6#v14)</sup> For sin shall not have dominion over you: for ye are not under the law, but under grace.

- “For sin shall not have dominion over you.” Remember, sin is pleasurable; we read it in Hebrews 11:24. Sin is fun, but that’s why we got to abstain from the things that our body’s telling us make us feel good. Don’t let that pleasure dominate your life; sin shall not dominate your life.
- I know it’s nice to look at porn. You looking at breasts and you keep looking: that’s all porn is. Porn is fornication. And all you brothers that say ‘I watch porn but I got it under control’: no you don’t. 1 Peter 2:11 shows how we know.
- “For ye are not under the law, but under grace.” You’re not under the law of animal sacrifice, but under grace, the grace of Christ, the New Covenant. That’s why Revelation 12 said we overcame by the blood of the Lamb.
- “For sin shall not have dominion over you: for ye are not under the law, but under grace.” Here they say: there you go, you’re not under the law. But after saying let not sin reign in your mortal body, would Paul now say we don’t have to keep the laws? That would make Paul, inspired by God, bipolar.

**[Romans 6:15](/bible/romans/6#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/romans/6#v15)</sup> What then? shall we sin, because we are not under the law, but under grace? God forbid.

- “What then? shall we sin, because we are not under the law, but under grace?” Shall we keep breaking the law because we are not under the law of sacrifice but under grace, under Christ? “God forbid”: that means no.
- “What then? shall we sin, because we are not under the law, but under grace? God forbid.” Those that’s in Christianity: shall we continue to sin because we are not under the law of sacrifice? God forbid you continue in sin, because Christ laid down his life for you once and for all. You don’t continue in sin no more; if they don’t learn it, they’ll see it when that second death comes.
- Christianity uses this scripture to say we’re not under the law anymore. But that statement alone tells you: shall we sin because we are not under the law? Sin is the transgression of the law, so it’s a law that is still being kept. It’s not talking about not keeping God’s laws; you keep God’s laws and you try to perfect the law. That’s what grace is about. Exactly.

**[Romans 6:16](/bible/romans/6#v16)**

- That’s why we got to take moral inventory, why you got to do self-examination: what am I struggling with, what’s my problem?
- “Whether of sin unto death”: sin unto eternal damnation, that’s the death he’s talking about. “Or of obedience unto righteousness”: meaning eternal life.
- You also got to find your purpose in this truth. “Know ye not, that to whom ye yield yourselves servants to obey, his servants ye are to whom ye obey; whether of sin unto death, or of obedience unto righteousness?” You become the servant of whom you obey. If you serve God, you’re God’s servant; but if you serve Satan, you’re Satan’s servant.

**[Romans 6:23](/bible/romans/6#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/romans/6#v23)</sup> For the wages of sin is death; but the gift of God is eternal life through Jesus Christ our Lord.

- “For the wages of sin is death”: we read that in Galatians 5:19, all the works of the flesh; the wages of sin is death. And it ain’t talking about regular death. That death is not the death that everybody goes through, that this flesh goes through; it is the damnation Christ mentioned in John 5:29. The wages of breaking God’s law is damnation, condemnation. It’s not oh I had a heart attack and I die; it ain’t talking about that.
- “But the gift of God is eternal life”: eternal life is immortality, celestial immortal power on the earth, “through Jesus Christ our Lord.” That’s what we all want, that’s what we all fighting for right there.
- “For the wages of sin is death”. Sin is the transgression of the law, and its wages are death, not eternal life. “But the gift of God is eternal life through Jesus Christ our Lord.” Eternal life is through Christ, and Christ said, if you love me, keep my commandments. What scripture will they give for obtaining eternal life another way?
- “For the wages of sin is death; but the gift of God is eternal life through Jesus Christ our Lord.” The payment for sin is death, and through Christ is eternal life. Isn't that what we are looking for? When we keep the commandments, what are we doing it for? The kingdom. God says that if you keep the commandments he is going to give you the kingdom, so you are expecting the kingdom.
