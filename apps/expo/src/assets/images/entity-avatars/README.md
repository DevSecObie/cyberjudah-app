# Entity placeholder avatars

Shown for a person or group that has no portrait (`src/features/lexique/strongEntityAvatars.ts`).

The 15 people avatars (`male-1` to `male-5`, `female-1` to `female-5`, `group-1` to `group-5`) were redrawn for CyberJudah on 2026-10-29 with Higgsfield (GPT Image 2.5, high quality) as 3D clay busts in the style of the earlier Bible Strong placeholders, which were used as references. People are Black, Hispanic or Native American; men have full beards with heads uncovered; women wear a head wrap. Exported as 256-pixel WebP at quality 80.

The eight object avatars (`language`, `musical`, `other`, `place`, `star`, `supernatural`, `time`, `title`) are unchanged from Bible Strong.
